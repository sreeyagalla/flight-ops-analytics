## Metrics

| Key | Label | Definition | SQL |
|---|---|---|---|
| `flights` | Number of flights | Count of scheduled flights, including cancelled. | `COUNT(*)` |
| `avg_dep_delay` | Average departure delay | Mean of departure delay in minutes (early = negative). Cancelled flights have no delay and are excluded. | `AVG(dep_delay)` |
| `avg_arr_delay` | Average arrival delay | Mean arrival delay in minutes. Flights with no arrival record are excluded. | `AVG(arr_delay)` |
| `on_time_rate` | On-time arrival rate | Share of flights with a recorded arrival that arrived <= 15 minutes after schedule. | `AVG(CASE WHEN arr_delay IS NULL THEN NULL WHEN arr_delay <= 15 THEN 1.0 ELSE 0.0 END)` |
| `cancellation_rate` | Cancellation rate | Share of flights with no actual departure recorded. | `AVG(CASE WHEN is_cancelled THEN 1.0 ELSE 0.0 END)` |

Grouped answers exclude groups with fewer than 100 flights.
